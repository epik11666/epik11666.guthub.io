About the designer

I am a game designer with hands-on experience across free-to-play mobile production and live operations, working in Unity and Unreal Engine on everything from deep idle and simulation economies to tight hyper-casual loops.

My approach centers on player-first thinking — every system, event, and level I design is evaluated through the lens of how it makes the player feel. Progression, pacing, economy balance, and flow are not afterthoughts; they are the design.

I work fluently across game & level design, economy and battle-pass balancing, GDD authoring, engine integration, and cross-team collaboration — comfortable preparing task breakdowns for programmers and artists, running A/B experiments, and driving iteration from first concept to shipped, data-tuned content.

A first-person psychological horror game about isolation and the slow erosion of reality — designed, built, and shipped to Steam entirely on my own.

Nullwake trades jump-scare spectacle for sustained dread: an abandoned hospital that stops behaving the way it should, corridors that run too long, and a town where reality quietly overlaps with something else. I built the experience around atmosphere over action — using pacing, space, silence, and environmental storytelling to hold tension, and letting the player notice what shouldn't be there instead of spelling it out.

As the only person on the project, I carried it across every stage — level and space design, encounter and pacing logic, environmental narrative, and the build and Steam release itself. It's the clearest demonstration of how I think about a whole game, not just a system inside one.
