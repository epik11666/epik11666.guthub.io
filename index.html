SECTION 1.0

Overview — About Me

this is the
whole job,
honestly ←

I am a game designer with hands-on experience across free-to-play mobile production and live operations, working in Unity and Unreal Engine on everything from deep idle and simulation economies to tight hyper-casual loops.

My approach centers on player-first thinking — every system, event, and level I design is evaluated through the lens of how it makes the player feel. Progression, pacing, economy balance, and flow are not afterthoughts; they are the design.

I work fluently across game & level design, economy and battle-pass balancing, GDD authoring, engine integration, and cross-team collaboration — comfortable preparing task breakdowns for programmers and artists, running A/B experiments, and driving iteration from first concept to shipped, data-tuned content.

Homa Games

← featured!
RoleGame DesignerPeriod01/2023 – 11/2025
TypeMobile Game Dev → Live-OpsSetupRemote

Worked as a Game Designer at Homa Games on All in Hole — a hybrid-casual black-hole title that reworked the classic arcade "hole" loop into a deep, level-based progression game that went on to break into the global top-50 grossing charts. Contributed across the full lifecycle: first through the core development phase that turned a single-mechanic prototype into a structured, metagame-driven product, then through sustained live operations. Operated fully remote as part of a distributed international team.

During development, helped design and specify the systems that turned the arcade core into a retention-driven game — level-based progression, the lives/energy loop, booster mechanics (Magnet, Chrono, Lightning), and the Royal-Match-style events metagame layered on top of core gameplay. Authored detailed game design documentation defining system behaviour, progression pacing, reward logic, and trigger conditions, used directly by engineers and artists for implementation.

In the live-ops phase, owned the design and balancing of the free-to-play economy and seasonal content — including the Treasure Pass (battle pass) seasons, Team Battles, currency sinks, and the recurring themed level packs (100 new levels per release) that keep the content calendar full. Tuned difficulty curves, reward tracks, and upsell moments to protect both progression feel and monetisation across a large, active player base.

Designed and documented A/B experiments across regions — testing tutorial flow, difficulty ramps, event configurations, and offer timing — then translated live KPI and cohort data into concrete balance and design changes each update cycle. Worked in a fast, data-driven pipeline where every progression and economy decision was validated against real player behaviour.
